At the end of the month it's too late

Your scholarship or part-time salary comes in forints, and money from family arrives in tögrög, euros or dollars. Rent, the BKK pass and groceries are in forints. You add it all up at the end of the month with a rate you googled, and only then find out the food money was gone by the 20th.

Plan, and get warned

Give each category a plan, and record what you spend in forints, tögrög, euros or dollars. The moment a category goes over its plan, a red warning appears.

Import your Mongolian bank statement

Pick the deposit statement (.xlsx) your Mongolian bank gives you. The app reads it in your browser, sorts each purchase by the shop's name into Food, Going out or Unknown, converts the tögrög to forints, and keeps transfers and money in apart from your spending.

Run it on your computer in 2 minutes

Budget Planner is not a website: it runs on your own computer, in your browser. You need Git and Node.js 18 or newer, on Windows, macOS or Linux.

No Git? Download the ZIP, unpack it, and run node server.js --demo in that folder.

Source on GitHub

14

  1. Copy the commands and run them in a terminal:

    git clone https://github.com/esst-prog2/budget-planner
cd budget-planner
node server.js --demo

  2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8766 in your browser.

  3. Under "Import a bank statement", pick demo/sample-statement.xlsx: a made-up statement in a real Mongolian bank's format, 30 transactions from August to November.

The demo starts empty every time. For your own budget, run node server.js and open http://127.0.0.1:8765.

How it's built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step.
  • A small Node server that serves the app and writes the CSV file.
  • The .xlsx statement is unzipped in the browser with its built-in DecompressionStream, so there is no spreadsheet library.
  • 80 automated tests (node --test), including the sample statement import, live rates and income in several currencies.
  • Planned with OpenSpec; every decision is in PLANNING_LOG.md and every step in PROCESS.md.
16

What it doesn't do (yet)

  • Bank statements from other banks: only the Mongolian deposit statement format is read.
  • Must-pays and plans are in forints only.
  • Categories are fixed: Food, Clothes, Gym, Subscriptions, Going out, Other, Unknown.
  • No savings goals, no phone notifications, no phone app: it needs a computer.
