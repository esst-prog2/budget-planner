Every currency in forints

Today's rates for tögrög, euro and dollar are filled in for you. If your bank's rate differs, type your own. Expenses in any of the four currencies are counted in forints.

Import your bank statement

Pick the statement .xlsx your bank gives you. The app reads it in your browser, sorts each purchase into Food, Going out or Unknown by the shop's name, puts each month in its own column, and keeps transfers and money in apart from your spending.

Fix a mistake, look back

Every expense, typed or imported, is in the History with its date and time. Delete a wrong one and the warnings and the report update at once. Pick any earlier month to see what you planned and what was left.

How it's built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step.
  • A small Node server that serves the app and writes the CSV file.
  • The .xlsx statement is unzipped in the browser with its built-in DecompressionStream, so there is no spreadsheet library.
  • 73 automated tests (node --test), including the sample statement import.
  • Planned with OpenSpec; every decision is in PLANNING_LOG.md and every step in PROCESS.md.
18

What it doesn't do (yet)

The budget is in forints only. Categories are fixed: Food, Clothes, Gym, Subscriptions, Going out, Other, Unknown. No savings goals, no phone notifications.
